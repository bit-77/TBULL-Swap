FAQ

Everything About
TBULL Swap

All your questions about swapping and staking on TBULL Swap, answered.

What is TBULL Swap?+
TBULL Swap is a TRON-native DEX aggregator and staking platform built around the TBULL token. It finds the best TRC20 token swap routes across all major TRON liquidity pools including SunSwap, JustSwap, SunStable and more — all in a single, non-custodial transaction. TBULL Swap also offers a staking module where users earn up to 42% APY in TBULL rewards.
What is the TBULL token?+
TBULL is the native utility and governance token of the TBULL Swap ecosystem, issued on TRON as a TRC20 token. TBULL holders benefit from reduced swap fees, staking rewards, and governance voting rights over protocol upgrades. The token is tradeable on TBULL Swap and all major TRON DEXes.
How do I swap tokens on TBULL Swap?+
Connect your TronLink wallet using the "Connect Wallet" button. Select your input TRC20 token (e.g., USDT) and your output token (e.g., TBULL). Review the quoted rate, estimated output, and route. Click "Swap Now" and approve the transaction in TronLink. Your swapped tokens arrive in under 3 seconds thanks to TRON's fast finality.
How do I stake TBULL and earn rewards?+
Navigate to the Staking section on TBULL Swap. Connect your TronLink wallet, enter the amount of TBULL you wish to stake, choose a lock period (7 days at 8% APY up to 365 days at 42% APY), review the reward preview and unlock date, then click "Stake TBULL". Rewards are distributed daily directly to your connected wallet and can be claimed at any time.
What is the maximum APY for staking TBULL?+
The maximum APY on TBULL Swap is 42%, available for the 365-day TBULL Diamond staking pool. Shorter periods offer 8% (7 days), 18% (30 days), and 30% (90 days). All rewards are paid in TBULL tokens and distributed daily to stakers. The longer you commit, the more you earn.
What fees does TBULL Swap charge?+
TBULL Swap charges a protocol fee of 0.05% on swaps — one of the lowest rates on TRON. TBULL token holders receive an additional 20% fee discount, reducing their fee to just 0.04%. Network bandwidth costs on TRON are negligible, often under $0.001. There are zero hidden charges; the displayed quote always includes all fees.
Is TBULL Swap safe and audited?+
Yes. TBULL Swap is fully non-custodial — your funds never leave your wallet until a swap executes atomically on TRON. All smart contracts have been audited by reputable security firms. MEV protection is built in to shield your transactions from front-running bots. If a swap cannot complete for any reason, tokens are automatically returned to your wallet.
What wallets are compatible with TBULL Swap?+
TBULL Swap is optimized for TronLink (browser extension and mobile app), which is recommended for the best TRON experience. It also supports TokenPocket, Trust Wallet (TRON network), WalletConnect-compatible TRON wallets, and imToken. No account or email registration is required — just connect your wallet and start swapping or staking.
Does TBULL Swap require KYC?+
No. TBULL Swap is a fully decentralized protocol. There is no account registration, no email, no identity verification (KYC), and no personal data collection of any kind. All you need is a compatible TRON wallet such as TronLink. Your wallet address is the only identity on-chain.
How does TBULL Swap find the best swap rate?+
TBULL Swap's routing engine simultaneously queries 120+ TRON liquidity pools including SunSwap V1/V2, JustSwap, SunStable, and all other active TRON DEX protocols. A pathfinding algorithm then splits your order optimally across pools to minimize price impact and maximize output. Quotes refresh every 5 seconds so you always act on live, accurate pricing.
Can I unstake my TBULL early?+
Early unstaking is possible but incurs a penalty fee to protect long-term stakers. The penalty ranges from 5% (for the 7-day pool) to 15% (for the 365-day Diamond pool) of your staked principal. Accrued rewards up to the exit point are still claimable. The Flex pool (no lock-up) can be exited at any time with no penalty whatsoever.
What is slippage and how should I configure it on TBULL Swap?+
Slippage is the acceptable gap between your quoted price and the final executed price, caused by market movement in the time between quote and confirmation. For stablecoin-to-stablecoin swaps (e.g., USDT to USDD), 0.1% is sufficient. For TBULL or other volatile tokens, 0.5% is recommended. For large orders during high volatility, use 1%. The "Auto" setting uses TBULL Swap's volatility model to pick the optimal tolerance automatically.
What is MEV protection and why does TBULL Swap include it?+
MEV (Maximal Extractable Value) bots watch public mempools and front-run swap transactions, forcing you to pay a higher price and extracting value from your trade. TBULL Swap routes all transactions through a private relay layer on TRON so bots cannot detect and front-run your swaps. This protection saves typical users significant value on larger trades, with zero extra steps required from you.
Does TBULL Swap support cross-chain swaps?+
Yes. While TBULL Swap is primarily optimized for TRON-native TRC20 swaps, it also supports cross-chain routes to and from Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum, Solana and more via integrated bridge protocols. A cross-chain swap automatically handles the source chain swap, bridge transfer, and destination chain swap in a single user action, though it takes slightly longer than native TRON swaps.
How are staking rewards calculated and distributed?+
TBULL staking rewards are calculated on a per-block basis using the stated APY for your pool tier. Every 24 hours, the protocol snapshot distributes earned TBULL rewards to all active stakers proportional to their stake. Rewards accumulate in a claimable balance visible in your staking dashboard, which you can withdraw to your wallet at any time without affecting your staked principal.
Can I get quotes without connecting a wallet?+
Yes. You can enter any token pair and amount on TBULL Swap to see live rate quotes, routing breakdown, price impact estimates, and fee previews without connecting a wallet at all. Wallet connection is only required when you are ready to execute the swap or stake your tokens. This lets you compare rates freely before committing.
What TRC20 tokens can I swap on TBULL Swap?+
TBULL Swap supports all TRC20 tokens with active liquidity on TRON, including TBULL, USDT (TRC20), TRX, USDD, SUN, JST, WIN, BTT, USDJ, and hundreds more. Any TRC20 token listed on a TRON DEX is automatically discoverable on TBULL Swap. If you have a contract address for a token not in our default list, you can paste it directly into the token search.
Is there a TBULL Swap mobile app?+
TBULL Swap runs as a fully responsive web application at tbull-swap.vercel.app and works seamlessly in TronLink's built-in browser on both iOS and Android. You can also access it via TokenPocket's dApp browser. A dedicated native mobile app is in development and will be announced on our official community channels.
How does TBULL Swap compare to SunSwap?+
TBULL Swap is an aggregator that routes through SunSwap (and all other TRON DEXes), meaning you get the best of every liquidity source in one place. Trading directly on SunSwap limits you to their pools only. TBULL Swap also adds MEV protection, multi-hop routing, cross-chain bridges, and an integrated TBULL staking module — features SunSwap does not offer. You always get a better or equal rate by using TBULL Swap.
How can I contact TBULL Swap support?+
Community and support are available through our Telegram group, Discord server, and Twitter/X page (links in the footer). For technical issues or bug reports, use the GitHub repository or the Bug Bounty program. Response times are typically under 4 hours on Telegram for urgent swap issues. Note that TBULL Swap team members will never DM you first or ask for private keys.
